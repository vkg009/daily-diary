Your Diary, as a Book
Load the JSON files exported from your iPhone diary. Every export merges into one continuous book — flip through your days like turning real pages.
On iPhone: Export → AirDrop or share the .json to this laptop → keep every export in one Diary Backups folder → select them all here (Ctrl+A / Cmd+A).
You can also just drag the files onto this window. Once loaded, the book is remembered — it reopens automatically next time.
